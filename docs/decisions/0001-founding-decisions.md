# 0001 — Founding decisions for the re-vision

Date: 2026-11-20. Decided by Rufus in the coordination session, on the defaults proposed in the [workstreams plan](../plans/2026-11-20-workstreams.md). Later decisions get one file each in this folder.

1. **Platform:** stay on Flowershow unless the *Faith in Mind* build shows something it cannot do.
2. **What may go live without asking: anything.** "No one is looking at the site at the moment… you can do anything to it." (Rufus) Agents merge and push to `main`, which is the live site. The live site is its own preview; no separate preview site is needed. Revisit when the site has readers.
3. **Rufus's notes are data:** one file per text, each entry a quoted passage plus an optional comment, shown as a highlight with a margin note. Built in milestone 2; *Faith in Mind* ships first on footnotes. Rufus has existing notes elsewhere, including on his personal website (e.g. on Aurelius's *Meditations*): find and bring them in.
4. **Texts:** the existing texts plus only a few new ones for now; the focus is building out the site and the pipeline, and getting the shortlist. **The Dhammapada is the next new text**: Rufus recalls a good open-source translation (possibly in his Zotero). Then Heart Sutra, Tao Te Ching, *Meditations*. *2026-11-20 (Rufus):* the Dhammapada translation is Thanissaro Bhikkhu's (Sujato's CC0 version kept as a parallel), and *Meditations* is Gregory Hays's; Rufus supplied both EPUBs.
5. **Copyright:** Rufus is "pretty liberal". Publish the best translation in full with its status stated; also look at Project Gutenberg and other open sources, and prefer an open translation where it reads well. Source files and Yogins of Ladakh stay in an excluded `library/` folder.
6. **Passage links:** the number a reader would cite, levels joined by dots (`/meditations#4.3`, `/faith-in-mind#3`). One page per text. An id never changes once published.
7. **Reader annotation:** Hypothesis trial early, our own prototype later, decided by Rufus on the two. Invite-only when it comes.
8. **Design:** the one thing that most needs Rufus's taste. Agents have a go through all the stages without waiting, and show him the living design page for input as early as there is something to react to. Reports That Matter is a sober reference to start from; Sutras should have its own distinctive design, which takes real work.
9. **URLs and layout:** texts keep their current URLs at the root; supporting files in folders. Red Pine's partial Diamond Sutra stays, marked partial.
10. **Name:** "Sutras" for now; renaming and a wordmark are parked.
11. **Reuse:** general pieces start here and are extracted only when another project uses them.

## How we work (Rufus, 2026-11-20)

Do not block on Rufus. Plans and designs are critiqued by a subagent (or another top-level agent such as Codex), the critique is integrated, then it is built, and the work keeps rolling. Make a judgement call and continue. Stop for him only on something big and hard to undo.
