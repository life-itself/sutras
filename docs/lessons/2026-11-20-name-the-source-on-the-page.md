# Name the source of each introduction fact on the page, not only in the notes

Date: 2026-11-20. Track: editorial.

The first eight introductions recorded their sources in `NOTES.md` (now `docs/editions/<slug>.md`), but a fresh critic reading only the page could not tell a date from Wikipedia from a date in the translator's own introduction, and rightly flagged a dozen sentences as unsourced. One sentence under Source and licence settles it: "In the introduction, X's dates and the book's history are from Wikipedia; what he says of his method is from his preface. Each is listed in the edition notes."

**Lesson:** the reader of the page is the audience for sourcing too. Keep the detail in the edition notes, but say on the page, in a sentence, where the kinds of fact came from.
