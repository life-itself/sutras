# Check the translator credit against the source, not the old page

From the Shodoka edition (2026-11-20). The old page said "Translated by Nyogen Senzaki and revised by Robert Aitken". The source named only Aitken, and a second copy (Terebess) said Aitken and Eido Shimano, 1960s, revised by Aitken in 1991. Senzaki's own 1953 version is a different text that shares some wording.

**Lesson:** treat every credit, date and title on an existing page as unverified. Find who made the translation in the source itself or a second witness, and record the trail in NOTES.md. A second copy of the same text is worth fetching: it confirmed two slips and supplied the translation's history.
