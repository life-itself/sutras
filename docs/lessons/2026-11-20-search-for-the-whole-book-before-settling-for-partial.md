# Search for the whole book before settling for a partial text

The shortlist said the Thich Nhat Hanh *Diamond Sutra* was a short version and Red Pine's had only 3 of 32 chapters, and that completing either needed Rufus's own copy of the book. Two searches (a distinctive phrase plus the translator's name, in "extended" mode) found both whole books as ebook PDFs on terebess.hu, the same site that supplied *Faith in Mind*, plus a chanting text of Red Pine's at the Austin Zen Center to use as a second witness. Both editions went live complete the same day.

So: before marking a text partial or asking Rufus for a book, search for the full text with a phrase from a missing part (e.g. "sands of the Ganges" for the Diamond Sutra), and check terebess.hu, archive.org and Buddhist centres' chanting PDFs. Record what was found, and its rights status, in the text's `NOTES.md`.
