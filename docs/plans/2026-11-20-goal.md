## Rules for every track

- Read `AGENTS.md`, this file, your workstream brief in `docs/plans/workstreams/`, and `docs/decisions/`. The model for an edition is `faith-in-mind.md` on `main` with `library/faith-in-mind/NOTES.md`, and `docs/guides/making-an-edition.md` once it exists.
- **Don't wait for Rufus.** Decide, note the decision on your bead, keep going. Have a fresh subagent critique anything substantial (an introduction, a page) before you call it done.
- **Ship small and often.** Commit only your own files (never `git add -A`). To ship: `git fetch origin && git rebase origin/main && git push origin HEAD:main`. `main` is the live site; check your page live after pushing. If you break the site, revert and push first.
- **Stay in your files.** If you need a change in a file another track owns (most often the shared template), don't make it: create a bead for that track saying exactly what you need, and work round it meanwhile.
- **Beads is the log.** Claim your bead (`bd update <id> --status in_progress`), comment what you did, close it when its done-check passes. Create beads for anything left over. Anything only Rufus can answer: a bead labelled `needs-rufus` with your recommendation and the default you took.
- **Editions:** keep the words exactly; every quotation verbatim; facts in an introduction only from a source you read; passage ids are the number a reader would cite, levels joined by dots, and never change once live; in-copyright translations are published in full with their status stated.
- When your track's done-check passes, write lessons (one short file each in `docs/lessons/`), then say so on your bead and stop.
