Sutras: a first mood board

Six existing things to look at, and two we are not. A small board on purpose: say which of these you feel something for, which leave you cold, and where to look further, and the next board widens from that. Behind this page sit a hundred candidates already captured and a separate page of five directions read from them; both change with what you say here.

Each image is captured at the moment that matters, which is often scrolled down from the top. Nothing is mocked up. Museum images are open-licence and credited.

Six references

1

Emergence Magazine: the opening of a poem

warm grey paper; one quiet image per text, an old landscape with one modern stroke; the long breath before the first line.a full screen of art before every page; the magazine nav; a slight sleekness.
2

Plum Village: a sutra page

the brushed name as the only ornament; the title with its source line under it; the text starting at once in a generous serif.the Play and Download buttons above the first line; the picture floated into the text; the yellows elsewhere on the site.
3

Co–Star: the black band

the nerve to leave a screen almost empty; three objects given a whole screen; one typeface for everything; texture from a faint line drawing, not colour.the cold, the centring, the knowing tone, the small text.
4

Kaikado: tea caddies in a row

how much of the page is empty; a grey ground rather than white; things in a row with room between them, which is how a short list of texts could look; one brushed mark.nothing much; it is a shop below this screen.
5

Zhao Mengfu: the Heart Sutra, with its readers' seals

black text as one continuous surface; red for nothing but the marks around it; the seals are literally the marks of earlier readers, which is what your notes in the margin are.copying it: vertical text, a scroll layout or a seal-script wordmark would be costume.
6

Fitzcarraldo Editions: the covers

one colour, spaced capitals, author and title and nothing else; a house style that never changes; each text as an edition with a cover, and the EPUB the same object.the shop: basket, breadcrumbs, books photographed in rooms.
