Sutras: mood board, widened

You loved Kaikado and the Emergence page, found Co–Star clean and its use of images charming (as inspiration, not a model), saw Fitzcarraldo as the cover for EPUBs and PDFs, set Plum Village aside as too warm and the seals as too far, and asked for something funkier. So this board keeps the three you loved and widens in three directions: more like Emergence and Kaikado, images for a textual site, and a funkier option. A colour strip carries the red-or-blue question.

Captured at the moment that matters, often scrolled; nothing mocked up; museum images open-licence and credited. The five directions page will be rewritten from what you say here.

More like Emergence and Kaikado

Quiet pages with one image given room, and objects or drawings on a pale ground.

Emergence: a drawn opening

a drawing rather than a photograph; grey on warm paper; art that reads as texture, not illustration.a whole screen of it before every page.

Emergence: the poem itself

Chinese above English as equals; a title in spaced capitals and an italic line for poet and translator; a narrow column with air.white rather than the warm grey of the opening; the nav.

Emergence: a diagram through the page

an old diagram as the one image; the title in two lines of small capitals.

Azmaya

one photograph, one pale colour inside it, the smallest possible nav. The Kaikado nerve again.

Nendo

hairline drawings as the only ornament on white; the wordmark barely there.

Images for a textual site

You said images would add something, and that scroll imagery is nice as pictures. Open-licence ink, calligraphy and objects could be the one image each text gets, in the manner of Emergence or Kaikado.

Sesshū, splashed ink

Sengai, three shapes

Hakuin, one character

A moon jar

All four could sit where Emergence puts its artwork: one per text, small, on a pale ground. The scroll you set aside (Zhao Mengfu's Heart Sutra) could return this way, as a picture rather than a page form.

A funkier option

Four pages with real personality, one from your own archive. The Poetry Project is the genuinely funky one; the others are colour and drawings. The question is whether any of this energy belongs on Sutras at all.

Dirt

colour used as a card, not a ground; a diagram where others put a photo; a sense of humour.most of it, for a sutra. It is a feed.

The Poetry Project

the nerve to make the whole page one sentence of type; nav as coloured words in prose.

Reading Design

a single saturated ground with one object on it; how far one colour can go.

Making Software (your archive)

drawings in one ink beside a text column; a pixel title as the only flourish. Geeky rather than funky, and the one page here that could carry a text.

Colour: the red-or-blue question

You like the red on the Emergence page and the Fitzcarraldo blue, and worry blue is cold. Three more ways one colour has been used on paper.
