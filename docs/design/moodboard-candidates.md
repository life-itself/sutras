# Mood board candidates

Everything scanned for the Sutras mood board on 2026-11-20, with one line on why it was kept or dropped. The test: is this truly beautiful, and would Rufus feel something looking at it? Relevant-but-plain was dropped. Captures were made with headless Chromium at 1440×900 (some scrolled to the moment that matters); museum images come from Wikimedia Commons and the Cleveland Museum of Art under open licences. **Kept** means on the board; **held** means captured and good but cut to keep the board at about 25; **dropped** means not beautiful enough, broken, or in the wrong register.

## Galleries scanned

## What ended up on the board

After an art-director review of the first cut ("where is the true beauty in this?", image by image), the board is: five directions, Ink and space (Sesshū's *Haboku Sansui* with Kaikado), The warm page (Emergence's Red Pine poem opening), The hand (Plum Village's sutra page), Red and black (Zhao Mengfu's Heart Sutra with the Doves Bible), Seeds of Renaissance adapted (the live home hero). The floor: Co–Star's black band and Reports That Matter's Challenger opening, demoted from directions. Eight details: Hakuin's Virtue, Sengai's three shapes, Emergence's seeds diagram, Emergence's Song dynasty verse (Chinese and English), the moon jar, the Jingoji Lotus Sutra, the 1558 Xylander Marcus Aurelius title page, Fitzcarraldo's covers. Three anti-references: Terebess, Daily Zen, Headspace. "Before": the live *Faith in Mind* page on laptop and phone.

Cut by the review and now **held**: the Emergence Song-poem verse as a direction image (white, nav-heavy; kept as a detail), Aldus 1501 (mostly border), the Diamond Sutra frontispiece (dense, the opposite of a frame that gets out of the way), the Cao Quan rubbing (a skewed photo, and a second night image for a light-only site), SuttaCentral (plain; its virtue, hidden numbers, cannot be seen), the Seeds design-system cover specimen (a metadata table on black).

Rufus asked (2026-11-20) for 5–10 directions at most, a few details, and a shortlist of 3–4, so everything marked **held** above stays in this file and off the page.
