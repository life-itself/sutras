Sutras: the direction, in three sample sites

From your reactions to the two mood boards: Emergence's Red Pine page as the base (warm grey paper, one image per text given room, a small label, the title, then the text in a narrow serif column), held to Kaikado's restraint, with open-licence ink of the Sesshū kind doing the image work. Colour from the three you named: the warm red, the Fitzcarraldo blue, gold on indigo. A mark in the character for wisdom, 慧, as an option. Fitzcarraldo for the covers. Nothing funky.

Below: the same direction built three times as a working sample site (home, Faith in Mind with its real notes, Seneca, About), differing by colour, type and the mark. Each is the live page's own markup with the live reading system underneath, plus one CSS file, so whichever is chosen goes straight onto the site.

You asked us to build and decide without you and keep a backup. The recommendation is at the end. A fresh agent's critique was applied before this was published: tracked capitals removed except the one label above the title, the image capped to half a phone screen so the title shows on the first screen, the shelf aligned, scans blended into the paper, the brush mark replaced by a printed one, and the Seneca and Meditations images corrected.

The mark

You imagined the character for wisdom. 慧 (hui, the usual translation of prajñā) at nav size and at favicon size, in each direction's treatment, plus three alternatives. All are type, no drawing yet; the name "Sutras" stays as the wordmark in the text face.

慧Sutras慧
A · printed, red

Noto Serif TC in the warm red. Printed, not brushed: a brush face would be the brush script the brief rules out.

慧Sutras慧
B · printed, in a blue box

Noto Serif TC, a hairline frame. Printed rather than brushed; cooler.

The night square. Strongest as a favicon, heaviest in the nav.

慧Sutras慧
Brushed, in ink

Ma Shan Zheng, in the text colour. Shown so you can see the brush version; the brief rules brush script out, and the critique agreed.

道Sutras道
道, the Way

If wisdom is too Buddhist for a library with Seneca in it: the Way is the word Faith in Mind and the Tao Te Ching share, and the Stoics have their own.

S.S.
No character

An italic initial in the text face. The quietest, and the only one that is not East Asian; the backup if a character feels like costume.

Fonts

One text face per direction, all open licence and on Google Fonts, all carrying the Pali and Sanskrit marks (ā ī ū ṭ ḍ ṇ ñ ś ṣ ṃ ḥ). Labels in Instrument Sans at 11px, tracked; Chinese in Noto Serif TC. The live site today is EB Garamond, which the brief named as the likeliest dull result.

Recommendation

A goes live; C is the backup, kept as a sample site for you to compare. B is set aside: on paper the blue reads as links and is as cold as you feared.

  1. A is the direction you reacted to most directly (the Red Pine page, Kaikado) with the one colour you liked on that page. It reads best on a phone of the three, and it asks the least of a text like Seneca, which has no ink painting of its own.
  2. C is the most distinctive and the most "sutra", and the gold-on-indigo front matter is a real idea; but it is ceremony, it makes Seneca wear a Buddhist robe, and gold is weak for numbers and notes. Kept as the backup, and its band could still come back as a treatment for the Buddhist texts only.
  3. Mark: 慧 printed in the warm red (A), your own idea. The critique's objection is fair and recorded: on Seneca's and Marcus's pages it claims them for the East; the wordmark alone, or 慧 only on the East Asian texts, are the alternatives to show you. Nothing is drawn yet; a drawn mark is a later job.
  4. What happens next: direction A's CSS is applied to the live site (custom.css and the home page, with the landing session), checked on all nine editions at phone and desk width, and shipped in small commits. The mood board and this page stay as the record.
