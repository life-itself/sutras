# First design page, 2026-11-20 (rejected)

The first attempt at the Sutras design page, kept for the record. Rufus rejected it: "mood board is weak. where is the true beauty in this… you have jumped too much into analysis… start with clear brief and moodboard." It batched feel, measured research, mood board and three directions on one page; the board was a row of small top-of-page screenshots chosen for relevance; measurement and feature analysis crowded the page.

What was kept from it: the measurements and the verse and margin-note findings went to [research-reading-features.md](../../research-reading-features.md); the feel notes and anti-references fed [brief.md](../../brief.md). The three directions (Bare, The read copy, Red and black) were made before any direction had been chosen and are not carried forward as such; the clusters on the new board supersede them.

`brief.src.html` is the source, `brief.html` the built page (shots inlined), `shots/` the screenshots.
