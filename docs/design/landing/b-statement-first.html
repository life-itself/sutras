Sutras

In brief A small library of wisdom texts, from Buddhist sutras and Zen poems to the Stoics. Each is made into an edition to read slowly: a clean page, a short introduction, a link for every passage, notes in the margin and an EPUB to download. Curated, not complete: each text is here because it is worth reading, and some carry Rufus Pollock’s own notes alongside, starting with Meditations.
