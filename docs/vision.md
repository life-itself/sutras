# Sutras — vision

Status: first draft, 2026-11-20, accepted by Rufus as the working basis; not formally signed off. From a visioning conversation with Rufus (bead sutras-m2v.1). The name "Sutras" is provisional (see [brand](brand.md)).

## In one line

A curated library of wisdom texts, made into beautiful editions to read online and download, with Rufus's annotations and thoughts alongside.

## What it is

- **A place for Rufus to create texts he really wants to read or has found useful.** Making his own edition of a text is the core act. Wisdom texts in general, not only Buddhist: key Buddhist sutras and Zen texts, Marcus Aurelius's *Meditations*, Seneca, the Tao Te Ching and the like.
- **My annotations and thoughts on them.** The site is an experiment in annotating text, possibly publicly and with others.
- **Possibly a concept wiki as well**, particularly for Buddhist ideas, in the manner of wilber-wiki (works, people, concepts).

## Who it is for

1. **Rufus first, always.** "I'm always serving myself here." Building and annotating the library is also his study of wisdom work and practice.
2. **People looking online for wisdom texts in a good format**, e.g. Aurelius's *Meditations* or the key Buddhist sutras.

## Why

- **To read well.** The texts in an elegant, beautiful form, online and as EPUB (and perhaps PDF).
- **To learn.** Annotating and linking the texts is how Rufus studies them.
- **To experiment.** This is the first experiment in the Unified Text and Open Literature direction: texts that are accessible, annotatable, linkable and downloadable. (guess: what works here can carry over to literature in general.)

## Priorities

Each text should be (not ranked):

- **Accessible**: a clean, beautiful reading page.
- **Annotatable**: Rufus's notes shown with the text, possibly publicly and with others.
- **Linkable**: stable links to a text (guess: and to passages within it).
- **Downloadable**: EPUB, perhaps PDF.

## What it is not

- Not an academic archive.
- Not a complete catalogue. It is curated: what Rufus finds interesting. It is not [terebess.hu](https://terebess.hu/) in comprehensiveness.

## Stance on copyright

Rufus is "an open-source revolutionary" here and is less concerned about copyright in general: publishing is not gated on permission. Where a translation is in copyright, showing samples (e.g. in a side-by-side comparison) is one option. The practical policy per text is still open (see the [plan question tree](plans/2026-11-20-sutras-scq.md)).

## Relatives

- **wilber-wiki** (`life-itself/wilber-wiki`): the pattern for a structured, AI-assisted knowledge base. Sutras is similar but more about publishing the actual texts.
- **Reports That Matter** (reportsthatmatter.org): turns hard-to-access reports into readable, searchable, linkable pages. Rufus sees strong analogies and wants to learn from it.
- **Unified Text / Open Literature** (Rufus's planning repo): the parent idea. Sutras is the narrower, wisdom-only first experiment.
- **Life Itself**: the home organisation and domain (sutras.lifeitself.org).

## Possible later features (not committed)

- Different versions of a text side by side (e.g. Tao Te Ching translations), at least as samples.
- A concept wiki, particularly for Buddhist ideas (guess: with verified excerpts, as in wilber-wiki).
- Annotation by and with other people.
