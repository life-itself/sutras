# Sutras — brand

Status: first outline, 2026-11-20. Mostly open; needs its own session. Built on [vision.md](vision.md). Visual identity is the job of the moodboard bead (sutras-m2v.3).

## Narrative

A few texts worth reading slowly, set beautifully, with one reader's notes in the margin. (proposed)

## Promise

- The text, clean and beautiful, online and to download.
- Curated, not complete: each text is here because Rufus finds it worth reading.
- Notes and thoughts alongside. (guess: clearly separate from the text itself)

## Audience

Rufus first. Then people looking online for wisdom texts in a good format.

## Voice

Open. (guess: plain, quiet and personal; a reader's notes, not a scholar's apparatus or a teacher's sermon)

## Name

Open. Rufus: "maybe we should have a better name than sutras", since the scope is wisdom texts in general. To decide: keep or rename. (guess: also whether it stays under lifeitself.org)

## Structure

Open; see bead sutras-m2v.4. (guess: texts, people, concepts, as in wilber-wiki)

## Visual identity

Direction from Rufus (2026-11-20); the moodboard bead (sutras-m2v.3) works it out.

- **Be the thing.** The brand should resonate with the very texts shown: "we want to *be* zen, to be the tao somehow in what we are building."
- **Beautiful text sites.** The text is the design.
- **Stripped down.** References: Reports That Matter and Co–Star (costarastrology.com), "but a little warmer".
- **A little more Plum Village**, though that "can get a little twee". So: warm, not cute.
- **Prior work:** Rufus's `rufuspollock/ux-patterns` repo has earlier work on beautiful text sites; read it before the moodboard.

From the [inspiration scan](2026-11-18-inspirations.md) (agent, not yet agreed): reading comfort as the main feature (serif body, narrow measure, quiet colour); a contemplative front door on an orderly structure.
