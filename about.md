## What it is

Sutras is a small library of wisdom texts: key Buddhist sutras and Zen texts, and others such as Seneca and Marcus Aurelius’s *Meditations*. Each text is here because Rufus Pollock finds it worth reading, or has found it useful, and wanted an edition of it he would really read.

## Who it is for

First, Rufus himself. In his words: “I’m always serving myself here.” Making and annotating the library is also his way of studying these texts and the practice they describe.

Then anyone looking online for a wisdom text in a good format: a clean page to read, and a file to take away.

## What each edition has

- **A clean reading page.** The text first, set to be read slowly, with a short introduction and a note on the translation.
- **A link for every passage.** Each verse, stanza or section has a number you can link to, such as [/faith-in-mind#3](/faith-in-mind#3). Once published, a number does not change.
- **Notes in the margin.** Editorial notes, and Rufus’s own notes beside the passages they are about, starting with [<em>Meditations</em>](/meditations).
- **A download.** Each text as an EPUB for an e-reader.
- **Its source.** Where the text came from and, once an edition is finished, how it was checked against that source word for word, with every change listed in the edition notes.

## Copyright

Where a good open translation reads well, we use it. Where the best translation is still in copyright, we publish it in full and say so plainly on the page, with how to reach us if you hold the rights. Our own additions (introductions, notes) are free to reuse under [CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/).

## What it is not

Not an academic archive, and not a complete catalogue. For a vast collection of Zen texts, see [Terebess Asia Online](https://terebess.hu/).

## Who makes it

Sutras is made by Rufus Pollock with AI assistants, as part of [Life Itself](https://lifeitself.org). It is also an experiment in texts that are easy to read, to link to, to annotate and to download, which might carry over to literature in general. The pages are Markdown, published with [Flowershow](https://flowershow.app). The [source is on GitHub](https://github.com/life-itself/sutras), where you can also [report a mistake](https://github.com/life-itself/sutras/issues).
